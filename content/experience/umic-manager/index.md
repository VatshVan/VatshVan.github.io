### Responsibilities & Milestones
* **Budget & Resource Allocation**: Solely responsible for managing and optimizing an annual operating budget of **INR 4+ Million**, allocating capital for automotive-grade sensors (LiDARs, RTK GNSS, industrial cameras), embedded compute platforms, and chassis fabrication.
* **Inter-Subdivision Architecture Alignment**: Bridged communication and system integration between the Technical Core (Perception, Planning, Controls, Mechanical, Electronics) and Non-Technical departments.
* **Recruitment & Talent Development**: Spearheaded a rigorous 2-month recruitment drive evaluating 100+ candidates across technical challenges, algorithmic interviews, and control system project assignments.
* **Competition Engineering**: Directed technical milestones, safety documentation, and operational protocols preparing the vehicle platform for premier national and international autonomous robotics challenges.
