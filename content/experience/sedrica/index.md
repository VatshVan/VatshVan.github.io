Cross-functional team of 30+ students under the guidance of **Prof. Archak Mittal**, developing India's first Level 5 self-driving car, customized for Indian roads.

### Research & Engineering Experience (Controls & State Estimation)
* **F1TENTH Autonomous Racing:** Engineering a `LibTorch`-`Acados` C++ runtime to deploy learned-dynamics **HyperMPC at a 100 Hz** control loop on a 1/10-scale racecar.
* **Adaptive Parameter Learning:** Developing a GRU-based Hyper-Param network with B-spline control encoding to learn horizon-varying tire and kinematic model parameters.
* **Autonomous Golf Cart Localization:** Building an autonomous localization stack for reliable outdoor navigation using high-precision **RTK GNSS**.
* **Joint Sensor Fusion (EKF):** Developing a Joint Extended Kalman Filter fusing RTK GNSS & IMU measurements to estimate vehicle 6-DOF pose, linear velocity & heading in real time.

### Positions of Responsibility (Team Manager)
* **Budget & Resource Management:** Responsible for effectively managing an annual operating budget of **INR 4+ Million**, allocating resources for optimal outcomes across sensors, compute, and hardware.
* **Team Leadership & Recruitment:** Coordinating communication between Technical Core & Non-Technical teams; spearheaded a 2-month recruitment drive for 100+ aspirants.
