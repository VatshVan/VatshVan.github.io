Developed a collaborative multi-agent artificial intelligence framework within a 7-member research team for autonomous sky survey data exploration, transient detection, and astrophysical hypothesis generation.

### System Architecture & Pipeline Highlights
* **Multi-Agent Cognitive Architecture**:
  - Implemented specialized autonomous agents assigned distinct observational roles (Survey Ingestion Agent, Anomaly Verification Agent, Literature Cross-Referencing Agent, Hypothesis Synthesis Agent).
  - Orchestrated fault-tolerant LLM tool-use routines utilizing **Google Gemini** coupled with live academic literature search and citation cross-verification.
* **Semantic Knowledge Graph (Neo4j)**:
  - Constructed a graph database in **Neo4j** linking observed sky coordinates, spectral profiles, and historical astronomical catalogs.
  - Deployed transformer-based embedding models with a **95% semantic similarity threshold** for automatic claim and observation de-duplication across hundreds of published papers.
* **High-Throughput Swift FITS Pipeline**:
  - Engineered an optimized pipeline in Swift to stream and process heavy **Flexible Image Transport System (FITS)** observation files from space and ground-based telescopes.
  - Extracted temporal, spectral, and photometric flux features at scale to feed downstream anomaly detection models.
