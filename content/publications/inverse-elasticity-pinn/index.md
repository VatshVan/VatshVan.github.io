### Abstract
Non-destructive evaluation of spatially varying elasticity fields in additively manufactured lattices presents significant mathematical challenges due to ill-posed inverse boundary value problems. We propose a Direct Hybrid Collocation Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) that embeds governing 2D Cauchy momentum balance equations into a composite 7-objective loss. By coupling 8 Digital Image Correlation (DIC) surface anchor measurements with over 10,000 Sobol low-discrepancy interior collocation points, our framework successfully reconstructs displacement, stress, strain, and Young's modulus fields without requiring internal destructive testing.
