I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at **IIT Bombay** with a Minor in **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** from the Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (CMInDS). My work focuses at the intersection of **quantitative finance**, **stochastic modeling**, and **autonomous control systems**.

### Research & Quantitative Background
As a **Research Consultant (Master Consultant)** at **WorldQuant BRAIN**, I develop systematic alpha strategies across global equity universes. Achieving an out-of-sample Information Ratio (IR) of **2.39** and submitting **320+ alphas** with fitness scores up to **6.47**, I engineer orthogonal signals using statistical validation, genetic optimization, and LLM-guided exploration pipelines. I was selected as one of 12 consultants globally for the Rising Stars cohort to attend the Global IQC'25 Finals in Singapore.

### Autonomous Systems Engineering
At **SeDriCa (UMIC)**, IIT Bombay's autonomous vehicle team developing India's first Level 5 self-driving car, I serve as both a **Controls & State Estimation Engineer** and **Team Manager**. I engineer C++ runtimes using `LibTorch` and `Acados` to execute learned-dynamics **HyperMPC at 100 Hz** on a 1/10-scale racecar, and design **Joint EKF** sensor fusion pipelines fusing RTK GNSS and IMU telemetry for full-scale autonomous vehicle localization.
